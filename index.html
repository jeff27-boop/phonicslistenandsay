Help & settings

Choose any number. Yellow stars show finished words. The moving waves show listening activity, not measured voice volume.

Allow microphone access first. If the permission prompt appears, release, allow access, then hold the microphone again.

Speech recognition may use your browser’s online service. No recordings are saved by this game. Gentle checking allows small vowel differences and common sound-alike spellings. G and C sounds still count separately. This is word recognition, not a pronunciation score.
